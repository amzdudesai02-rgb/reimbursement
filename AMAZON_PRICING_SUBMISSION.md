# Amazon App Listing - Pricing Information

## Pricing Model: **PAID SERVICE**

This service uses a **Subscription + Commission** pricing model.

## Pricing Plans

### **Starter Plan (Launch Plan)**
- **Monthly Subscription**: $29.99 per month
- **Annual Subscription**: $299 per year (saves 2 months)
- **Commission**: 10% of recovered amount
- **Volume Range**: Up to $30,000 per month in recoveries
- **Ideal For**: New or small sellers testing automation

### **Growth Plan** ⭐ (Recommended)
- **Monthly Subscription**: $69.99 per month
- **Annual Subscription**: $699 per year (saves 15%)
- **Commission**: 
  - 9% of recovered amount (monthly billing)
  - 8% of recovered amount (annual billing)
- **Volume Range**: $30,001 – $100,000 per month in recoveries
- **Ideal For**: Growing sellers scaling operations

### **Enterprise Plan**
- **Monthly Subscription**: $149.99 per month
- **Annual Subscription**: $1,399 per year (saves 20%)
- **Commission**: 7% of recovered amount (negotiable for high volume)
- **Volume Range**: $100,000+ per month in recoveries
- **Ideal For**: Large or multi-account sellers
