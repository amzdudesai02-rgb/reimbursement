# Quick Start: Accessing SP-API Developer Central

## ✅ Correct Path (Updated)

The SP-API Developer Central is **NOT** at `developer.amazon.com`. It's accessed through **Seller Central**.

### Step-by-Step Access:

1. **Log into Seller Central**
   - Go to: https://sellercentral.amazon.com
   - Use your seller account credentials

2. **Navigate to Develop Apps**
   - Click on **"Apps & Services"** in the top navigation
   - Click on **"Develop Apps"** from the dropdown
   - OR go directly to: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/home

3. **You should now see:**
   - Developer profile section
   - Your applications list
   - Option to "Add new app client"

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

### Wrong:
- ❌ Going to `developer.amazon.com` (this is for Alexa/Appstore)
- ❌ Looking for "Developer Central" in Settings
- ❌ Using the Amazon Developer console

### Correct:
- ✅ Use Seller Central → Apps & Services → Develop Apps
- ✅ The URL should be `sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/...`

---

## 📝 What You'll See

Once you're in the correct section, you should see:

1. **Developer Profile** section
   - Complete your profile if not done
   - Submit for approval

2. **Your Applications** section
   - List of your SP-API apps
   - "Add new app client" button

3. **Roles & Permissions**
   - Request access to Finances, Reports, etc.

---

## 🔗 Direct Links (After Login)

- **Develop Apps Home**: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/home
- **Create New App**: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/register/new

**Note**: These links require you to be logged into Seller Central first.

---

## 🆘 If You Still Can't Find It

1. **Check your seller account type**
   - You need an active Seller Central account
   - Some account types may not have access

2. **Check permissions**
   - Your user account needs permission to develop apps
   - Contact your account administrator if needed

3. **Try different regions**
   - US: https://sellercentral.amazon.com
   - UK: https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk
   - EU: https://sellercentral.amazon.de (or other EU marketplaces)

4. **Contact Amazon Support**
   - Seller Central Help → Contact Us
   - Ask about SP-API Developer access

---

## ✅ Verification

You're in the right place if you see:
- "Develop Apps" in the page title
- "Add new app client" button
- SP-API mentioned (not Alexa or Appstore)
- Your developer profile section
