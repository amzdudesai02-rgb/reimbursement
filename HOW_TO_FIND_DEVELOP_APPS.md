# How to Find "Develop Apps" in Seller Central

## 🎯 You're Currently On:
- **Selling Partner Appstore** (where sellers browse/install apps)
- This is NOT where you create apps

## ✅ Where You Need to Go:
- **Develop Apps** (where developers create SP-API applications)

---

## 📍 Navigation Steps:

### Method 1: Through Apps & Services Menu
1. Look at the **top navigation bar** in Seller Central
2. Find **"Apps & Services"** in the menu
3. Click on it to see a dropdown
4. Look for **"Develop Apps"** or **"Develop Your Apps"** in the dropdown
5. Click on it

### Method 2: Through Settings
1. Click on the **gear icon** (⚙️) in the top right
2. Look for **"User Permissions"** or **"Account Settings"**
3. Navigate to **"Developer"** or **"API Access"** section
4. Look for **"Develop Apps"** or **"SP-API"** link

### Method 3: Direct Search
1. Use the **search bar** at the top of Seller Central
2. Type: **"Develop Apps"** or **"SP-API"**
3. Look for results that say "Develop Apps" or "Develop Your Apps"

### Method 4: Through URL (After Login)
Try these URLs directly (you must be logged in first):

**US Marketplace:**
```
https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/home
```

**Alternative URLs to try:**
```
https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop
https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/register
https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/apps
```

---

## 🔍 What to Look For:

When you find the correct page, you should see:
- ✅ "Develop Apps" or "Develop Your Apps" in the page title
- ✅ "Add new app client" or "Create new application" button
- ✅ Your developer profile section
- ✅ List of your existing applications (if any)
- ✅ SP-API mentioned (not Appstore)

---

## ❌ What You're Currently Seeing:
- "Selling Partner Appstore" (for browsing apps)
- "Search apps" bar
- Categories and resources

This is the **wrong section** - you need to find the **developer section**.

---

## 🆘 If You Still Can't Find It:

### Check Your Account Type:
1. **Professional Seller Account**: Should have access
2. **Individual Seller Account**: May have limited access
3. **New Account**: May need to wait 24-48 hours after account creation

### Check Permissions:
1. Your user account might not have developer permissions
2. Contact your account administrator
3. You might need to request developer access

### Alternative: Contact Amazon Support
1. Click **"Help"** in the top right of Seller Central
2. Select **"Contact Us"**
3. Ask: "How do I access the Develop Apps section to create an SP-API application?"

## 🎯 Exact Steps (Try This):

1. **From the Appstore page you're on:**
   - Look at the **top navigation bar**
   - Find **"Apps & Services"** (usually near Settings/Help)
   - Click it → Look for **"Develop Apps"**

2. **If you don't see "Apps & Services":**
   - Click the **hamburger menu** (☰) on the far left
   - Look for **"Apps & Services"** or **"Developer"** section
   - Navigate from there

3. **Try the direct URL:**
   - Make sure you're logged in
   - Copy and paste: `https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/develop/home`
   - Press Enter

---

**Note**: The exact menu structure can vary by account type and region. If none of these work, you may need to contact Amazon Seller Support to enable developer access for your account.
