## 🚨 **Step-by-Step: Contact Amazon Support**

1. **Go to Seller Central:**

   - Navigate to **Help** → **Get Support**

2. **Select Support Category:**
   - Choose: **"Apps & Services"** or **"Developer Support"**
   - Sub-category: **"Selling Partner API"**

3. **Create Support Case:**
   - Subject: **"SP-API App Review Status - ReimbursementDash (Submitted for 2+ weeks)"**


### **Method 2: Through Developer Central**

1. **Go to Developer Central:**
   - From Seller Central: **Apps & Services** → **Develop Apps**

2. **Find Support Option:**
   - Look for **"Help"** or **"Contact Support"** link

3. **Submit Request:**

## ⏰ **Timeline Expectations**

- **Normal Review:** 3-7 business days
- **With Questions:** 1-2 weeks (Amazon waits for your response)
- **Extended Delay:** 2+ weeks usually indicates:
  - Backlog on Amazon's side
  - Missing information they're waiting for
  - Additional security review needed

## ✅ **After Contacting Support**

1. **Save the Support Case ID/Number**
   - Reference it in future communications
   - Check back in 2-3 business days for response

2. **Continue Checking:**
   - Email daily (including spam)
   - Developer Central for status changes
   - "Edit pending review" for new requirements

3. **Follow Up if No Response:**
   - Wait 3-5 business days
   - If no response, escalate with case reference number
